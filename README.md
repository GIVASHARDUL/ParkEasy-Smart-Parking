# 🚗 ParkEasy — Smart Parking Prototype

### A Modern Parking Facility Concept by Team NOVA | Yukthi 2026

**ParkEasy** is a 3D smart parking prototype developed by **Team NOVA** for Yukthi 2026. The project explores how thoughtful parking-facility design and technology-inspired concepts can improve parking organization, vehicle access, and the overall parking experience.

Developed using **Blender**, the prototype presents a modern parking garage with organized parking bays, vehicle placement, entry and exit barriers, directional signage, and presentation lighting.

Our vision is to establish a foundation for smarter, more efficient parking solutions that address everyday parking challenges.

## 🌟 Project Overview

Finding suitable parking can be time-consuming, particularly in busy locations where parking spaces and vehicle access points are difficult to navigate.

ParkEasy explores a more organized approach through the visual design of a modern parking facility. The 3D model demonstrates how structured parking bays, designated entry and exit routes, barrier gates, and clear signage can contribute to a better-organized parking environment.

The current implementation is a **visual 3D prototype** designed to communicate the concept, layout, and proposed vehicle-access arrangements.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Parking facilities can become difficult to navigate when parking spaces, traffic flow, and entry and exit points are not clearly organized.

ParkEasy aims to explore a structured parking-facility design that emphasizes:

- Better organization of parking spaces.
- Clearer vehicle entry and exit arrangements.
- Improved visual guidance through signage.
- A foundation for future technology-assisted parking solutions.

## 🧭 Project Objectives

- Design a modern parking garage in a 3D environment.
- Visualize organized parking bays and vehicle placement.
- Demonstrate designated entry and exit points using barrier gates.
- Improve the presentation through signage, materials, and lighting.
- Establish a foundation for future smart-parking functionality.

## 🚀 How to Explore the Prototype

1. Open this repository and locate the ParkEasy `.blend` project file.
2. Download the file to your computer.
3. Open it using a compatible version of Blender.
4. Switch to the saved camera view to inspect the intended composition.
5. Explore the garage, parking bays, vehicles, signage, and entry and exit gates.
6. Play the timeline to inspect any animations included in the saved project.

**Note:** Rendering performance may vary depending on your computer's hardware and Blender settings.

## ⚙️ Current Scope

The current deliverable focuses on the **3D design and visual demonstration** of the ParkEasy parking-facility concept.

The prototype illustrates the proposed layout and access arrangements. Real-time parking-slot detection, live availability updates, automated vehicle recognition, and hardware-controlled barriers are not claimed as implemented features.

## 🔮 Future Enhancements

Potential directions for future development include:

- **Live Parking Availability:** Display available and occupied parking spaces.
- **Smart Slot Detection:** Explore sensor-based or computer-vision-assisted occupancy detection.
- **Vehicle Recognition:** Investigate automated vehicle identification at entry and exit points.
- **Digital Dashboard:** Develop an interface for viewing parking availability and facility status.
- **Automated Access Control:** Explore integration with suitable gate-control hardware.
- **User Experience Improvements:** Investigate parking guidance and slot-selection features.

These enhancements represent future development possibilities, not current prototype capabilities.
